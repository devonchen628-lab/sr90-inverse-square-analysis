Far-field comparison of the Geant4 air-to-vacuum response ratio and the effective attenuation inferred from experimental model M3. (a) Both responses are normalised at the 30 cm rail position; the far-field selection begins at 5 cm, from a 1% analytical finite-geometry criterion. Geant4 bands show pointwise 16–84% whole-batch bootstrap intervals; the narrower M3 band is a formal profile interval conditional on the experimental model. (b) Geant4 minus M3, with pointwise conditional 16–84% intervals combining the Geant4 bootstrap and formal experimental uncertainty. Both responses decrease overall, while Geant4 falls more strongly at the longest distances; this supports qualitative agreement but does not establish quantitative agreement. The intervals do not include uncertainties in source geometry, materials, threshold or response models; the vacuum and analytical-geometry responses also have different distance dependences.
