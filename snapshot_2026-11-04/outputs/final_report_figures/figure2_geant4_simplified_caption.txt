Geant4 air-to-vacuum detector-response ratio and the experimental M3 fitted attenuation term, each normalised to unity at 30 cm rail separation. All 32 simulation positions are shown; vertical error bars show pointwise 16–84% batch-bootstrap intervals. The light-grey region marks distances excluded from the attenuation fits by the predefined 1% analytical geometry criterion. At large separations the simulated response declines more strongly than the experimental fitted term. The dashed blue line is the anchored far-field exponential summary of the simulated ratio, which is not required to follow an exact exponential law. The red curve represents a model fit rather than directly measured experimental transmission.
