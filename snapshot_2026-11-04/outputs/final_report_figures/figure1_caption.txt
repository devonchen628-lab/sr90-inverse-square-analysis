Experimental model comparison. (a) Measured count rate multiplied by the square of the rail separation, $Rd^2$, with propagated counting and individual distance-reading uncertainties (one standard deviation). M0 is the fitted constant baseline; M2 includes finite source–detector geometry, background and a conditional non-paralyzable dead-time response; M3 additionally includes effective exponential attenuation. (b) Standardized residuals for M2 and M3, calculated using counting and independent distance-reading uncertainties, reveal remaining distance-dependent structure despite the improvement under M3. Shared offset, response-model uncertainty and fitted-parameter correlations are not included in these pointwise residual denominators. The fitted M2 offset is in tension with its independent constraint. The M0 constrained fit requires implausibly large distance pulls; its line is an exploratory reference rather than a validated fit.
